Chapel TSC

The Chapel Technical Steering Committee (TSC) is the group that formally governs the Chapel language project. Though most project decisions are made using an informal consensus-based approach, those related to governance, or requiring a more formal vote, are decided by the TSC. For further information about the committee, including a list of its current members, please refer to the project’s GOVERNANCE.md file.
